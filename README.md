# Capstone 1: Maine Sales Territory Analysis

SQL and Excel analysis of in-store sales for the **Maine** territory.

▶️ **Video:** [Watch here](https://youtu.be/GavF7FCcL18)

## Files
- `capar_sales_analysis.sql`: queries, results and recommendation
- `capar_territory_chart.xlsx`: PivotChart of monthly revenue by year
- `capar_capstone1_presentation_v2.pptx`: slides used in the video

## Key Findings
- Total sales: **$1,877,249.75**, growing from $279K (2022) to $695K (2025)
- Maine is **7.75%** of Northeast sales (4th of 4 states)
- July–December was stronger than January–June every year
- **Technology & Accessories** leads in transactions and average sale

## Recommendation
Use the slower Q1 2026 months to prepare for back-to-school: plan campaigns early and stock Technology & Accessories at South Portland.
